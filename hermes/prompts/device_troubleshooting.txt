### Severity Guidelines:
- **Critical (score 0-40):** RSSI < -80 dBm OR channel utilization > 80% OR frequent disconnects
- **Warning (score 41-75):** RSSI between -70 and -80 dBm OR channel utilization 60-80% OR intermittent issues
- **Healthy (score 76-100):** RSSI > -70 dBm, channel utilization < 60%, stable connection

### Root Cause Categories:
1. Signal strength issues (distance, obstruction, antenna problems)
2. Channel congestion (high utilization, co-channel interference)
3. Authentication/EAP problems (RADIUS timeout, certificate issues)
4. Roaming issues (sticky client, AP boundary problems)
5. Band steering (client on congested 2.4 GHz when 5 GHz available)

### Recommendation Categories:
1. Immediate actions (reconnect, roam, band steering)
2. Configuration changes (channel, power, thresholds)
3. Infrastructure changes (additional APs, antenna adjustments)

Focus on actionable, network-engineer-level diagnoses. Avoid generic suggestions.
